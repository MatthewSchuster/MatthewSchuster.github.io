Common Excersises

The following are common common excersises that we encourage our clients to do as part of their fayly exersise routine.

Burbee
Burpees are great exersises to improve your strength and endurance. Begin in a standing position, drop into a squat and extend your hands forward, kick your feet back and than forward again quickly, and than jump up from a squated position.
Plank
Planks build your core strength. To perform a plank, get into a pushup position and rest your forearms on the floor. Hold the position for as long as you can.
Mountan Climber
Mountan Climbers are a good cardio exercise. Place your hands on the floor in a pushup position, than bring one knee up to your chest and switch as quickly as you can (as though you are climbing a mountan).
